## 누가 썼고 왜 모범 사례인가

- **저자** Gergely Orosz. Uber·Skyscanner에서 엔지니어링 매니저로 이력서 수백 장을 심사했고, 뉴스레터 *The Pragmatic Engineer*를 쓴다. 2020년 5월 무료 이력서 리뷰를 제안했다가 300건 넘게 몰리자 공통 피드백을 14쪽 PDF로 만들었고, 그것이 215쪽 책 *The Tech Resume Inside Out*이 됐다.
- **검증** 책 기여자 17명이 Shopify·Coinbase·Square·Uber·Airbnb·Disney Streaming 등의 하이어링 매니저와 리크루터다. **심사하는 쪽이 쓴 문서**라는 점이 다른 이력서 조언과 다르다.
- **채택 규모** 템플릿 다운로드 6,500회 이상(소개 글 기준). r/EngineeringResumes 위키([02-engineering-resumes-wiki.md](02-engineering-resumes-wiki.md))가 채용 파이프라인·ATS·섹션 구조 설명을 이 책의 무료 장으로 링크한다.
- **한계** 유료 장은 확보하지 못했다. 5장 Resume Structure, 6장 Standing Out, 13장의 사례 5건(백엔드 6년차, SRE 20년차 포함)이 여기 해당한다. 다만 원본 14쪽 가이드가 4~6장의 축약판이어서 규칙 자체는 거의 다 들어 있다.

## 1. 템플릿 뼈대 (.docx 본문에서 추출)

템플릿은 **경력자용 순서**다. 학생·신입은 아래 "템플릿 메모"대로 Education을 위로 올린다.

| 순서 | 섹션 | 담는 것과 템플릿의 지시문(원문) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 머리글 | 이름 · 도시, 국가 · 전화 · 이메일 · LinkedIn. 한 줄이다. |
| 2 | 요약 (선택) | 예문 *"I am a full-stack software engineer especially interested in building magical mobile experiences, scaling systems up, and shipping reliable applications."* 메모: *"Note: use the summary when it makes sense."* |
| 3 | Work Experience | 항목 머리: 직함 · 회사 · 기간 / 팀 · 도시. 불릿 지시문: *"Results, impact and your contribution. Add numbers to the impact, use active language and mention specifics like the languages used. **The more recent the position, the more details you usually want to share.**"* 과거 직장: *"As the position is more in the past, you'll want to be mindful on how much details you add, and what you call out. You want to make sure there's space on all the key details on the first page."* 인턴: *"The further in the past a position is, often the less important its details."* |
| 4 | Technologies and Languages | 세 줄. `Languages:` `Technologies:` `Other:` (예: Data structures and algorithms) |
| 5 | Education and Certifications | 학위, 학교, 국가, 기간. 한 줄씩 |
| 6 | Projects | *"Projects are worth mentioning when they are standout, and when they add to your resume. When you have strong and relevant work experience, they are less important. **Describe the impact, the challenges and why the project stands out.** Blogs and articles can also be projects."* 예: *"Technical blogging on The Pragmatic Engineer blog. Popular articles include a piece on Angular Material and one on Clean Code."* |
| 7 | Other Sections | Volunteer work(Projects와 합치기 권장) · Patents · Awards(*"especially relevant when you have less work experience"*) · Certifications and Courses(*"projects show off skill far better than a course"*) |
| 8 | Interests | *"Listing interests can be a conversation starter. Don't go overboard on this one though."* |

**템플릿 메모(원문)**

- *"Order of the sections could be different. This template is optimized for people with work experience. For students, new grads and bootcamp grads, you'll probably want to move the Education part up, and focus on project work, when you don't (yet) have much work experience."*
- *"You might want to move Technologies & Languages to the top, below the summary - depending on the type of companies you are applying for."*
- 색과 글꼴은 바꿔도 된다. *"This is a template that is a starting ground for your resume."*
- 소개 글의 경고: 템플릿의 이메일을 안 바꾸고 낸 사람이 있어 면접 초대가 저자에게 갔다. **자리표시자는 전부 바꿀 것.**

## 2. 원본 14쪽 가이드의 규칙

### 2.1 The First Glance — 리크루터가 첫 눈에 찾는 다섯 가지

*"Recruiters want to collect a few key pieces of information at first glance: and it is in your best interest to make this easy. If recruiters can't find this information, and there's lots of resumes to go through, they might move on to the next one."*

1. **Years of experience.** 졸업 연도를 보고 경력 연수를 계산해 내부 레벨(예: L4 = 3~5년)과 맞춘다. 찾기 어려우면 탈락 더미로 간다.
2. **Relevant technology skills.** 공고의 기술(예: Java·Go)이 보이는가, 어느 숙련도인가. *"don't count on this"* — 다른 언어로 미루어 짐작해 주리라 기대하지 말 것.
3. **Work experience.** *"Do you come across as someone who has consistently delivered impact?"*
4. **Work authorization and visa status.** 해외 지원 시 취업 허가 여부를 명시하지 않으면 "비자 필요" 더미로 밀린다.
5. **Anything that clearly stands out.** 신입은 학교나 수상, 경력자는 회사·특허·PhD·오픈소스 핵심 기여.

*"So how do you make this information stand out? You make sure most information is on the first page, and you use clear formatting, and good use of colors and bolding to draw attention to the relevant parts."*

### 2.2 Ground Rules — 말 안 해도 지켜야 하는 것

1. *"Good grammar and NO typos."* 오탈자는 주의력 부족으로 읽힌다.
2. *"Contact details."* 이메일, 전화, 거주 도시·국가만. 전체 주소는 불필요.
3. *"Dates in reverse chronological order."* 최신이 위.
4. *"Don't include photos or non-required personal information like your date of birth, gender, citizenship, relationship information, number of children, religion, or others."*

분량: *"Your resume should be two pages or less."* *"For new grads and people with a few years of experience, fit in on one page. If you have less than a few years of experience, it's not expected you fill in the second page."*

### 2.3 Simplicity and Consistency

1. 일관된 서식과 글꼴 크기.
2. 오탈자 없음(반복 강조).
3. *"Bullet points for easy readability. Use bullet points that make the CV easier to read. Avoid paragraphs."* 하위 불릿은 피한다: *"They clutter your resume, make it more verbose, and make it harder to read. If you find yourself using these, re-edit your resume and stick with one level."*
4. 날짜: *"06/11 - 07/12"* 는 읽는 사람이 생각하게 만든다. *"June 2011 - July 2012"* 로 쓴다. 4~5년 넘은 기간은 월을 빼도 된다.
5. *"PDF format. Use this format and no other."*
6. *"Be concise, and don't spell out trivial things. Ruthlessly edit your resume and drop sections that add little to no information. Ask yourself: 'will the person reading the resume get information that helps them assess if I am qualified for this job?'"*

### 2.4 Avoiding Biases

*"Do not add personal details to your resume that can lead to negative bias during the resume screen."* *"For most resumes, you do not need more than your name and your e-mail address to apply."* 사진: *"all hiring managers and tech recruiters I've spoken to confirmed that photos add no value."*

### 2.5 Results, Impact and Your Contribution — 불릿을 쓰는 공식

*"When listing your work and project experiences, focus on **what you achieved, as opposed to what you did**. For the achievements, try to quantify these with the impact and (business) results. A framework you could use is **"Accomplished {impact} as measured by {number} by doing {specific contribution}"**. This is similar to the structure Google encourages for resumes."* (공식의 원출처는 [04-xyz-formula-laszlo-bock.md](04-xyz-formula-laszlo-bock.md))

세 가지 편집 지침:

- **Use numbers.** *"Most resumes do not contain numbers: if you add these specifics, you will stand out."* 숫자의 종류: 팀 인원, 코드 줄 수, 커버리지 전후, SLA 변화, 매출, 사용자 수, 설치 수, 별점 수, 해결한 티켓 수.
- **Use active language.** *"Use active verbs like 'led', 'managed', 'drove', 'improved', 'rolled out' over passive ones like 'improving' or 'rolling out'."*
- **Mention specific languages and technologies towards the end of your description.** *"Impact and your contribution are more important to convey than the technologies."* 본문에 쓴 기술은 기술 섹션의 목록과 겹쳐야 한다.

전후 대조(원문):

| Original statement | Edited for results, impact and specifics |
|---|---|
| Worked on the Payments team, developing microservices. | Improved the availability of the payout Go microservice from 99.8% to 99.9% by improving the monitoring/alerting system and proposing, implementing and rolling out a read-through cache layer using Redis. |
| Automated error handling process of our restful API services that reduced one of our clients support ticket rate. | Reduced support tickets by 80% for a large client by stepping up to rework error handling, ensuring 100% of error codes are mapped to appropriate HTTP codes, and non-mapped errors trigger exceptions on the Node.JS restful API. |
| Enhanced customer experience of the MySap platform through integrations of third party APIs such as zoom-rooms for remote meetings, square to handle recurring payment, etc. | Improved customer conversion by 30% of the MySap co-working platform by implementing recurring payments and integrating Zoom rooms, using PHP and Go. |

*"You stand out from the crowd by talking about the impact of your work and how you contributed to it, not just what you did. ... developers who follow directions are a dime a dozen."*

### 2.6 Don't be Humble

- *"Talk about yourself, not your team. Avoid using 'we' and use the first person instead (in most cases, you can drop the 'I')."*
- *"Be concise but not humble. Don't hide your achievements - and when in doubt, inflating them on the borderline will hurt less than hiding them."*
- *"Make your side projects & open-source contributions shine. ... Follow the results-impact-contribution model in calling this out, where you can, as opposed to just linking to your Github profile with no explanation or an app you've built. If you don't sell it, the person reading the CV might not look at it in detail."*
- *"Do talk about extracurricular activities, at the end of your resume. ... If you have a technical blog, link a specific, high-quality article, the reader can read."*

### 2.7 Write a Resume for That Job — 마스터본과 맞춤본

*"Create a 'master' version of your resume that lists out lots of details in your work experience and projects sections. ... Don't worry if this version goes beyond two pages - as long as you'll be able to trim it down for each job description. Then, create a version of your resume for the specific job description, re-editing it, so it uses similar language to what is in the job details. Remove examples that don't help you with this position, or move them out of the way."*

Amazon 결제팀 공고에 맞춘 예시. **내용은 같고 표현만 공고의 언어를 따라간다.**

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Languages: Python, PHP, Java, Go | Expert in Java and Go, proficient in Python and PHP |
| Other: data structures, algorithms, full stack software design | Other: Data structures, algorithms, distributed systems, engineering best practices |
| Designing and developing back-end systems with different tech stack (Java, Python, Go) | Defined, designed, implemented and rolled out a multi-tier customer profile service, using Java, leading a team of four engineers. This service is used by five other teams. |
| Created a generator of Grafana dashboards from microservice code / Developed a reverse proxy for testing | Introduced best practices on reliability, monitoring and alerting. Built a Grafana generator that creates dashboards from microservice code: this tool was adopted by more than 10 teams. Improved testing practices by developing a reverse proxy for testing, caching calls to test-environment, adopted by my team. |
| Introduced a beta environment, where new features can be roll-out for a selected amount of customers | Improved how we do rollouts by introducing a beta environment, where new features can be rolled out for a selected amount of customers. Rolled out this environment company-wide. |

### 2.8 Different Companies, Different Focus

*"Top tech companies care far less about the specific languages used, but they do care about software engineering skills. Consultancies and agencies are more interested in very specific technologies and years of experience with those technologies. Tailor your resume for each."*

### 2.9 채용 파이프라인의 숫자 (무료 장 *The Hiring Pipeline*)

- 이력서 스크린: *"The recruiter or inbound sourcer scans your resume, spending **5-20 seconds** to determine if you might be a match for the position."*
- *"**The dropoff is usually the steepest at this step.** From hundreds of resumes, only a few dozen profiles tend to have a recruiter screening call."*
- *"It's common for a funnel that started with 100+ qualified resumes to result in a single offer."*
- 심사자의 역할: 하이어링 매니저(최종 결정, 공고 작성), 리크루터, 소서, 인바운드 소서(대기업의 서류 스크리너), 코디네이터, 면접 패널. 리크루터와 인바운드 소서는 *"fundamentally on their side"* — 채용해야 목표를 채우기 때문이다.

## 3. Common Mistakes — 무료 장 전체 요지와 점검표 12개

네 묶음: **Poor Format**(다단 레이아웃은 훑기 어렵다, 굵게는 날짜·직함·회사·머리글에만, *"A developer CV is 95% about the content, 5% about the style"*), **Forgetting About Your Audience**(내부 약어·프로젝트명 금지, 공고에 맞추기, *"team player and a fast learner"* 같은 상투어 대신 근거, 장황함 금지), **Unnecessary Details**(사진, 연락처 4개 초과, 구사 언어, 기술 자가 평점, 추천인, 추천사), **Links**(클릭 안 되는 링크, 방치된 GitHub·사이트, 전체 URL 노출, 튀는 링크 색).

점검표(원문):

1. **Do you have a photo on your resume?** Remove it.
2. **How many contact details and social profile links do you have on your resume?** You should have no more than four. Do you need all of these?
3. **Do all the links work on your resume?** Click through each of them.
4. **Do all the links point to professional pages on your resume?** Ensure well-formatted, professional content.
5. **Is your resume easy to scan?** Give your resume to a friend and ask them to take a look at it for 5 seconds, then tell you their immediate impression.
6. **Is your resume simple enough?** Are you using any "fancy" designs? If so, is this kind of "fanciness" getting in the way of readability?
7. **Is the resume concise and to the point?** Are you using short sentences? Do you have bullet points with rarely more than two lines?
8. **Are you bolding too much?** Aim to not bold anything inside a sentence. Only bold important things like job title, company name, date, or headers.
9. **Is formatting consistent?** Are you using standard font sizes, and aligning with tabs, over spacing things to be approximately close?
10. **Are you using internal company jargon?** Do you have project names that only people inside the company would understand? If so, consider changing them to describing the project.
11. **Do you have a (spoken) languages or a references section?** You can probably remove both of these.
12. **Do links stand out with a different color or formatting?** They shouldn't. They take away space and attention.

GitHub 링크에 대한 경고(원문): *"There are few more disappointing things than seeing that your GitHub is untouched for years, your pinned repos do not have READMEs or that your website was last updated 4 years ago."*

## 4. 이 레퍼런스에서 가져가는 뼈대 요소

규칙으로 정리한 것은 [../rules-resume.md](../rules-resume.md)에 있다. 이 문서에서 나온 항목만 적는다.

- 섹션 뼈대와 순서 규칙(경력자 vs 신입), 요약은 선택.
- 불릿 공식 *Accomplished {impact} as measured by {number} by doing {contribution}* 과 편집 지침 3개(숫자·능동 동사·기술은 끝에).
- 첫 눈에 찾는 5가지가 첫 화면에 있어야 한다.
- 마스터본 1개에서 공고별 맞춤본을 만든다. 표현을 공고의 언어로 바꾸되 내용은 바꾸지 않는다.
- 점검표 12개는 제출 전 체크리스트로 그대로 쓴다.
