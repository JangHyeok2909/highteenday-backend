## 누가 썼고 왜 모범 사례인가

- r/EngineeringResumes는 엔지니어(소프트웨어 포함) 이력서 첨삭에 특화된 가장 큰 공개 커뮤니티다. 위키는 운영진이 관리하며, 게시 전 필독을 강제한다(*"your submission will get taken down if you do not read this wiki"*).
- 위키 스스로 기준의 출처를 밝힌다. 채용 파이프라인·ATS·섹션 구조 설명은 Gergely Orosz의 책([01-pragmatic-engineer.md](01-pragmatic-engineer.md)) 무료 장을 링크하고, 불릿 공식은 Google의 XYZ([04-xyz-formula-laszlo-bock.md](04-xyz-formula-laszlo-bock.md))를 링크한다. 즉 이 위키는 **여러 1차 출처를 한 장의 규칙표로 압축한 것**이다.
- 적용 범위를 스스로 한정한다: *"the guidelines provided in this wiki predominantly align with the standards of resumes in the United States and Canada."* 그리고 *"there are no clear-cut rules ... general suggestions and guidelines rather than definitive rules."*
- 미확보: 위키가 링크하는 실제 합격 이력서 이미지(imgur)와 "success story" 게시물 본문. 로그인 없이 열리지 않는다.

## 1. 서문 — 이력서의 정의

*"Your resume is essentially a **30-second elevator pitch on paper**, encapsulating your contact information, education, experience, and background. Its purpose is to convince the hiring team to interview you."*

*"Ultimately, your resume can only do so much if you don't have **genuine, relevant experience**."*

## 2. General Rules

### 읽기 쉽고 훑기 쉽게 (원문 12개)

1. Use a single-column layout
2. Avoid icons, images, and graphics.
3. Don't indent your sections and bullet points. Using bullet points inherently creates indentation
4. Use bullet points, not paragraphs.
5. Use a modern, easy-to-read font like Calibri, Bitstream Charter, or Arial.
6. Use a black (not thin) font with at least 10.5+ font size.
7. Don't justify your text since it creates inconsistent spacing between words
8. Ensure you have enough white space. No cramming. Use sufficient margins (minimum 0.4 inches) and line spacing (minimum 1.07).
9. Avoid excessive *italicization*, **bolding**, and ALL CAPS. If you do use one, use it independently of each other.
10. Have clear section separation.
11. **One page long, unless you have some 10+ years of experience. The rule of thumb is 1 page per decade of experience.**
12. Separate your skills using **commas**

### 효과적으로 (원문 4개)

1. Include relevant and technical details to showcase your qualifications and distinguish yourself from other candidates.
2. Provide context and incorporate relevant keywords.
3. Avoid adding unnecessary information that doesn't add to your candidacy.
4. **Bring your critical and pertinent details to the top and the left of the resume.** This increases the odds of these details being read by the hiring team.

### 왜 1쪽인가

- *"The majority of resumes that are posted on this sub don't need a second page. Once it gets cleaned up (content and format), a lot of people will have trouble populating 1 page with relevant, technical content."*
- *"The hiring team is going to spend far less effort on reading longer resumes."*
- *"If you're having trouble doing this with 1 page, consider removing content that is less relevant to the role you're applying to, even if it's technical. **You can add this to your portfolio instead.**"*
- *"If you decide to go with a second page, you should be in the position to at least be considered for a senior/staff role."*

## 3. 날짜 표기

- 현재 진행은 **Present** (~~Current, Now, Ongoing~~)
- 월을 쓰기로 했으면 계절·학기와 섞지 않는다: **March 2022**, not ~~Winter 2022~~
- 일(day)은 쓰지 않는다. 연도는 네 자리(**2023**, not ~~'23~~). 숫자 월(~~9/2013~~) 금지
- 약어 월은 표준대로: Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, **June**, **July**, Aug, **Sept**, Oct, Nov, Dec. 마침표 없음
- 기간은 en dash(–) 양쪽에 공백: **Mar 2012 – Mar 2022**
- 날짜는 오른쪽 여백에 정렬. 불릿이 날짜보다 오른쪽으로 나가지 않게

## 4. Section Order — 상황별 섹션 순서

| 상황 | 순서 |
|---|---|
| 졸업 후 정규직 재직 중 | *Work Experience > Skills > Education* 또는 *Skills > Work Experience > Education* |
| 학생·신입, 경력 적음 | *Education > Work Experience > Skills* 또는 *Education > Work Experience > Projects > Skills* |
| 기술 경력 없음 | *Education > Projects > Work Experience > Skills* |
| 경력 전무 | *Education > Projects > Volunteer Experience/Extracurriculars > Skills* |

- *"Do **not** include a summary/profile unless you're a senior/staff engineer or above, making a career change, or addressing an unemployment gap/returning to the workforce"*
- *"Do **not** include a references section"*

## 5. Contact Information

- 시민권·취업 허가가 불분명하면 이름 옆에 명시(예: "US Citizen").
- 주소·우편번호 금지. 도시도 해당 도시 공고가 아니면 생략(비지역 편향).
- LinkedIn 불필요(*"nobody will ever click on your LinkedIn"*). 전화번호도 불필요(리크루터가 이메일로 먼저 묻는다).
- 이메일은 **1개**, 현대적 제공자(Gmail/Outlook).
- **비어 있는 GitHub 링크는 넣지 않는다.**
- 이메일·GitHub·포트폴리오는 **마스킹하지 말고 평문**으로: `name@gmail.com` · `github.com/username` · `myportfolio.com`. "Email:", "GitHub:" 접두어 금지, `https://www.` 금지, 밑줄·색 금지.

## 6. Work Experience

- 섹션 이름은 *Work Experience* 또는 *Experience* (~~Professional Experience, Employment History~~ 등 금지)
- **유급** 경력만(연구 경력 포함). 인턴·계약직은 그렇다고 표시.
- 항목과 불릿은 **관련성 또는 인상 순**으로. *"put your best stuff first!"*
- *"**Tailor your resume** for each application so it addresses the job description. Show that you can do the job"*
- *"**Add context.** Simply listing positions/projects and adding industry buzzwords is not enough. You need to tell hiring managers what you did and how you solved problems."*
- *"**Differentiate yourself and don't be humble.** ... your resume is not your job description. Tell us about your accomplishments, tangible metrics, and technical victories."*
- *"**Avoid centering your skills around a piece of software if you can.** ... try to focus on the engineering skills behind it."*
- 신입: *"focus more on showing a mastery of fundamental engineering skills first, then worry about the project management/leadership stuff if there is room left."*

## 7. Bullet Points

### 목적 (원문)

1. Highlight the technical work you did
2. Highlight technical challenges you faced and overcame
3. Highlight the impact of your work

### 일반 규칙 (원문)

- Bullet points should be 1-2 lines long.
- Aim for **1** sentence per bullet
- Bullet points should be ordered from most relevant/impressive to least
- Don't use personal pronouns i.e. *I, we, us, my, our, their*
- Don't end bullet points with periods. Bullet points != sentences
- Don't spill bullets onto the following line with only 1–4 words on it.
- Avoid the excess use of **sub-bullet points**
- Avoid using apostrophes **'**, ampersands **&**, and slashes **/**
- Avoid the excessive use of adjectives and adverbs: ~~excellent, innovative, expert, revolutionary~~ / ~~creatively, diligently, meticulously, strategically, successfully~~
- Use digits instead of spelling out numbers: **8** ~~eight~~

### 동사

- *"Each bullet should begin with a **strong, past-tense action verb**"*
- **Good:** analyzed, architected, automated, built, created, decreased, designed, developed, implemented, improved, optimized, published, reduced, refactored
- **Bad:** aided, assisted, coded, collaborated, communicated, executed, exposed to, gained experience, helped, participated, programmed, ran, used, utilized, worked on
- **과장·군더더기 동사:** amplified, conceptualized, crafted, elevated, employed, engaged, engineered, enhanced, ensured, fostered, headed, honed, innovated, mastered, orchestrated, perfected, pioneered, revolutionized, spearheaded, transformed
- **자주 오용:** leverage, enhance, utilize

### 공식 — STAR, XYZ, CAR 중 하나

- **STAR:** Situation, Task, Action, Results
- **XYZ:** Accomplished **[X]** as measured by **[Y]**, by doing **[Z]**
- **CAR:** Challenge, Action, Result
- *"If you're able to quantify your achievements/results, **move the metrics towards the start** of each bullet"*

### 내용이 안 떠오를 때 스스로 묻는 질문 (원문)

**What did I do?** Did I solve a problem? Did I work on a team or work independently? How many people were on my team?
**How did I do it?** Why did I do it / why did I do it that specific way? Did I use any tools or resources to help me? Did I do any testing to validate my design choices? How did that testing impact my final design?
**What were the results of my actions?** Did I deliver my project on time or under budget? Was I successful with the design/solution/project? Did it actually work the way I wanted it to? What went wrong? Did I learn anything from the experience?

### Why Integration Matters

*"Don't throw a parts list at the reader. It matters less what specific components and subcomponents you used and more how you integrated all this into whatever your project was aiming to accomplish."* *"How did your software interface with the stepper motors to complete task X to a desired level of precision? That's the kind of stuff that stands out."*

### 소프트웨어 샘플 불릿 (`sample-bullet-points.md` 원문)

- Developed a back-end web service to handle user authentication using JWT and interacting with existing user services to store session data in Redis cache, leading to a 14% reduction in dropped session complaints
- Led the development of a data pipeline platform using Kafka streams, ingesting data from various data stores from across the application, resulting in a more streamlined developer experience for data query teams and reduced congestion by 24% during peak hours
- Developed a React Native-based mobile application by collaborating with product design teams, interacting with a GraphQL API allowing users to navigate and make orders to local restaurants
- Constructed and optimized distributed data scraping and processing pipelines leveraging Flume in Python and C++ to create labeled image datasets used for model training and evaluation
- Wrote backend video processing (C++) and video playback rendering code (Java, JavaScript) to support optimized video encoding/decoding which varied depending on operating system and platform and allowed for significant savings in video start latency and size

## 8. Education

- 수강 과목은 넣지 않는다(극히 특수한 경우 제외). 고등학교 금지. 학위 못 받은 학교 금지.
- 시작일 없이 **졸업(예정)일만**: *Expected May 2025* / *May 2021* 또는 *2021*
- GPA는 3.75 이상일 때만, 소수 둘째 자리. 첫 정규직 이후엔 뺀다.
- 학교 이름에 지명이 있으면 위치 생략. 장학금·수상은 극히 인상적일 때만.

## 9. Skills

**목적**: *"highlight in a few words what technical skills you can bring to the job. Ideally, these match the job description."* *"This is not a section to spew every buzzword on every technology you've ever touched."* 섹션 이름은 **Skills** 하나.

**넣을 것**: *"languages you've used thoroughly and could theoretically interview in"* · 실제로 써 본 프레임워크·도구 · **불릿에 나온 것과 서로 겹치게**.
**넣지 않을 것**: 소프트 스킬("teamwork", "leadership"), 당연한 것(타이핑, Word, IDE), 코드 호스팅 사이트("Git"은 되고 "GitHub"은 안 됨), 운영체제, IDE·편집기.

**형식**: **3줄 이하, 한 단**. 중요한 순서. 범주로 나눔(Languages, Technologies 등). **쉼표**로 구분. 고유명사 표기 정확히(SolidWorks, LabVIEW). "C/C++"가 아니라 "C, C++". 굵게 금지. **"Expert in", "Professional in" 같은 수식어 금지** — 숙련도는 불릿으로 증명한다. 세로 나열·다단 금지.

신입의 질문에 대한 답: *"An inexperienced engineer that lists a few skills they've really honed in on can often look better than a new engineer with 10+ skills they've clearly never used in industry."*

## 10. Projects

- 섹션 이름은 **Projects** (~~Personal/Academic/Selected Projects~~ 금지)
- 개인·동아리·취미 프로젝트용. 회사 프로젝트는 아님.
- 제목에 "project"라는 단어를 넣지 않는다. "Personal Project", "Group Project" 표시도 불필요.
- 역할·위치·날짜보다 **포트폴리오 페이지나 GitHub 저장소 링크**가 중요하다.
- 불릿으로, 문단 금지. 관련성·인상 순.
- *"they should be real projects, not mandatory school projects or trivial tutorial projects found online. Something that someone uses to solve a problem. Something that has users (can be just you, as long as you use it often) and is actively maintained and isn't just rotting in a GitHub repo."*

## 11. Portfolios (이력서에서 링크할 때)

- *"Include links to GitHub profiles and portfolio websites only if they are up-to-date. If your pinned repositories lack READMEs or if your portfolio hasn't been updated in a significant time frame, it's advisable to save space and omit them."*
- *"Only publish projects with good READMEs on GitHub. Aim for each README to have a good summary, screenshots, details on how to run, and **how to test**."*
- *"Include tests for your project that can be run. Have automated tests such as unit tests or end-to-end tests."*
- 참고: https://github.com/matiassingers/awesome-readme
- 전체 URL 금지: `github.com/bob`, `portfolio.com`

## 12. Bias

*"Don't include personal details to your resume that can lead to negative bias during the resume screen. This includes age/gender/children/nationality/ethnicity/marriage status/religion/political views"*

## 13. 시니어(10년+)·전직자 규칙 (참고)

시니어: 2문장 이내 요약 고려, 관리직·IC 이력서 분리, 2쪽 이내, 영향력(influence)도 언급, 옛 경력은 압축, 학력은 맨 아래.
전직자: 2문장 요약(전직 사유), 동작하는 프로젝트와 소스 링크, 1쪽.

## 14. Grammar, Punctuation, and Spelling

- 생소한 약어는 처음에 풀어 쓴다: "scanning tunneling microscope (STM)" (풀어 쓴 쪽은 소문자).
- 철자 검사가 아니라 **문법 검사**. 고유명사만 대문자.
- 도구: Grammarly, Hemingway Editor, LanguageTool, Quillbot. 친구·가족 교정.

## 17. 이 레퍼런스에서 가져가는 뼈대 요소

- 상황별 섹션 순서표(4절). 요약 없음 원칙과 그 예외 3가지.
- 불릿 규칙: 1~2줄, 한 문장, 과거형 강동사, 대명사·마침표 없음, 수치는 앞으로, 금지 동사 목록.
- Skills 3줄·쉼표·수식어 금지, 불릿과 교차 검증.
- Projects는 링크가 역할·날짜보다 중요. README에 실행법과 **테스트 방법**.
- 1쪽 원칙의 근거: 안 들어가는 것은 이력서가 아니라 **포트폴리오로 보낸다**.
